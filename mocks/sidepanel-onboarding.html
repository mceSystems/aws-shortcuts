Step 1 — Multi-session

Enable multi-session

AWS lets you keep up to 5 accounts open at once. AWS Shortcut relies on this so switching accounts won't sign you out of the others.

console.aws.amazon.com
aws Provide feedback Multi-session ▾ EN ▾
  1. 1Open console.aws.amazon.com.
  2. 2Top-right → click Multi-session.
  3. 3Choose Turn on multi-session.
Step 2 — Connect

Connect your SSO portal

Paste your AWS access portal URL. AWS Shortcut uses it to read your account list and to launch the console with the right role.

Access portal URL Looks like https://<your-org>.awsapps.com/start. Find it in any AWS account-switcher email or your SSO admin.
  1. 1Sign in to the portal in a browser tab.
  2. 2Make sure you can see your account list there.
  3. 3Come back here and continue.

Onboarding mock — notes

Three steps reuse the existing popup flow at side-panel dimensions (380×700). Sticky bottom action bar swaps Cancel/Back/Continue per step.

  • Step 1 stays unchanged — still drives the user to flip the multi-session toggle in the AWS console.
  • Step 2 is the SSO portal URL form. Auto-fills any existing stored URL.
  • Step 3 streams scan progress from the SW (already implemented in the popup version).
  • Once Step 3 finishes the panel swaps to the Main UI without a page reload — same component tree, just different React state.
  • If the user closes the side panel mid-onboarding, opening it again resumes at the right step (driven by stored prefs).
  • Settings page can also surface a "Reset onboarding" button for users who want to redo it.
